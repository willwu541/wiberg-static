Molded FRP grating (open mesh)

Molded FRP grating for chemical, marine, and aggressive environments. Lightweight, corrosion-resistant, and durable for platforms, walkways, and flooring.

Product Description

Molded FRP (fiberglass-reinforced plastic) grating is manufactured by molding resin and glass fibers into a grid pattern. It offers excellent corrosion resistance, lightweight construction, and low maintenance for chemical plants, water treatment facilities, marine environments, and food processing. Molded grating has a smooth top surface with good slip resistance and is available in various mesh sizes and thicknesses. Non-conductive and non-magnetic.

Specification Details
Manufacturing Molded resin and glass fiber; continuous mesh pattern
Surface Smooth top; optional grit surface for enhanced slip resistance
Mesh sizes Various; e.g., 38mm × 38mm, 25mm × 25mm; confirm per load
Resin types Polyester, vinyl ester for chemical resistance
Benefits Lightweight, corrosion-resistant, non-conductive, low maintenance
Typical use Chemical plants, water treatment, marine, food processing

Frequently asked questions

Is molded FRP bidirectional?

Yes in typical square mesh molded panels; verify load axes with the manufacturer table.

Can FRP support vehicle loads?

Only when heavy-duty pultruded or specialized systems are engineered for the wheel patch—do not extrapolate from light walkway tables.

Fire performance?

Product- and resin-specific; obtain ratings for escape routes.

Stainless clips with FRP?

Common; ensure isolation if galvanic rules apply.

UV exposure?

Surfacing and resin type must suit outdoor UV.

Compared to steel economically?

Weigh first cost against coating maintenance for the actual environment.
