FRP Grating

Corrosion-resistant FRP grating for chemical, water treatment, marine, and aggressive service environments where lightweight performance and low maintenance are important.

Fiberglass-reinforced plastic grating offers durability and corrosion resistance for demanding environments.

Product types

  • Molded FRP Grating
  • Mini Mesh FRP Grating

  • Corrosion-resistant for chemical and marine environments
  • Lightweight for easier handling and installation
