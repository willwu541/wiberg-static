Standard Trench Covers

Standard trench covers for drainage and utility channels. Load-rated for pedestrian and light vehicle traffic in industrial and municipal applications.

Request a Quote Downloads Product Spacing Guide

Product Description

Standard trench covers are fabricated from steel grating to span drainage channels, utility trenches, and access zones. They provide load-bearing capacity suitable for pedestrian and light maintenance vehicle traffic, with good drainage and open area for water flow. Available in welded construction with frame options, standard spacing formats, and hot-dip galvanized or painted finishes. Specify span, load requirement, and channel dimensions for quotation.

Specification Details
Construction Welded steel grating; frame optional per project
Load rating Pedestrian and light vehicle; confirm load and span for specification
Materials Carbon steel, stainless steel (304, 316)
Finishes Hot-dip galvanized, painted, bare, stainless
Typical use Drainage channels, utility trenches, plant access zones
Panel sizes Cut-to-size per channel dimensions and span

Common Specifications (GB & US Standards)

Trench covers to GB/YB/T 4001.1 and ANSI/NAAMM. Common spans: 300–1200 mm (GB); 12″–48″ (US). Load ratings for pedestrian (P) or light vehicle (H). Specify channel width, span, load, and standard in RFQ.
