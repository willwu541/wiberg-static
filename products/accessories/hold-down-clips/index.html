Hold-Down Clips

Hold-down clips for grating panel retention and anti-lift. Secure panels against uplift and movement in industrial platforms and outdoor applications.

Request a Quote Downloads Installation and Fixings

Product Description

Hold-down clips are designed to prevent grating panels from lifting or shifting under load, wind, or thermal expansion. They clamp or engage the grating from above or at the edge to provide positive retention. Hold-down clips are especially important for outdoor installations, elevated platforms, and areas subject to uplift forces. Available in carbon steel or stainless steel to match grating material and finish.

Specification Details
Function Prevent panel uplift and lateral movement
Typical use Outdoor platforms, elevated walkways, areas with wind or thermal movement
Materials Carbon steel, stainless steel (304, 316)
Finishes Hot-dip galvanized, painted, stainless
Installation Bolted to support; used with saddle clips or as standalone retention
Spacing Per project specification and load requirements
