Introduction

Marine and offshore environments demand corrosion-resistant grating for platforms, walkways, and access areas. Wiberg offers hot-dip galvanized steel grating and FRP grating for saltwater exposure, offshore platforms, ships, and coastal facilities.

  • Offshore platform flooring
  • Ship and vessel access
  • Dock and pier walkways
  • Coastal facility platforms
  • Drainage and trench covers
