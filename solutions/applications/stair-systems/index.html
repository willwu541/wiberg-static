Introduction

Stair systems with steel grating treads provide safe vertical access in industrial facilities, plants, and commercial buildings. Wiberg stair treads offer open drainage, anti-slip surfaces, and durable construction for high-traffic stairways.

  • Industrial stair access
  • Platform and mezzanine stairs
  • Outdoor stair systems
  • Wet or oily environment stairs
  • Emergency egress stairways
