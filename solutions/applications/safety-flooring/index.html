Safety Flooring

Anti-slip safety flooring and grating for high-risk areas, wet environments, and industrial applications.

Request a Quote View Products

Introduction

Safety flooring solutions provide anti-slip surfaces for high-risk areas including wet, oily, or contaminated environments. Wiberg serrated steel grating and FRP grating offer enhanced traction and durability for industrial safety applications.

  • Serrated bar grating for anti-slip
  • FRP grating for chemical resistance
  • Wet and oily environment applications
  • Food processing and chemical plants
  • Outdoor walkways and platforms
