Introduction

Industrial walkways provide safe, durable pedestrian access across factory floors, process areas, and outdoor sites. Wiberg steel grating walkways offer open drainage, ventilation, and anti-slip options for wet or oily environments.

  • Factory floor pedestrian paths
  • Process area access routes
  • Outdoor industrial walkways
  • Wet or oily environment access
  • Equipment perimeter walkways
