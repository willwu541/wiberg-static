Drainage Covers

Trench covers and drainage channel covers for industrial floors, process areas and municipal applications.

Request a Quote View Products

Introduction

Drainage covers protect trenches and channels while allowing water and fluids to flow through. Wiberg trench covers provide safe pedestrian and vehicular access over drainage systems with open-mesh steel grating for debris passage.

  • Industrial floor trench drainage
  • Process area channel covers
  • Pedestrian and vehicular access
  • Outdoor drainage systems
  • Wastewater and chemical drainage
