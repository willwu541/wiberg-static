Export Service

We support overseas customers with drawing-based quotation, project communication, export packing, and documentation. Responsive support for international industrial and infrastructure projects.

What We Offer

  • Project communication –– Clear, timely responses in English
  • Export packing –– Packing suitable for sea and air freight

  • Responsive support –– Typical response within 24 business hours
