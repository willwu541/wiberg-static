Chemical Resistant FRP Grating

Chemical-resistant FRP grating with vinyl ester resin for aggressive chemical environments. Ideal for chemical plants, wastewater, and corrosive applications.

Product Description

Chemical-resistant FRP grating uses vinyl ester or other chemical-resistant resins to withstand aggressive chemical exposure, acids, alkalis, and corrosive atmospheres. It is the preferred choice for chemical processing plants, wastewater treatment facilities, pulp and paper mills, and marine environments. Vinyl ester resin offers superior chemical resistance compared to standard polyester. Available in molded construction with smooth or grit surface. Confirm chemical exposure for resin selection.

Specification Details
Resin Vinyl ester or chemical-resistant resin; confirm per chemical exposure
Chemical resistance Acids, alkalis, solvents, corrosive atmospheres; confirm compatibility
Typical use Chemical plants, wastewater, pulp and paper, marine, aggressive environments
Surface Smooth or grit top; anti-slip options available
Benefits Superior chemical resistance, lightweight, non-conductive, low maintenance
Specification Provide chemical exposure for resin and laminate recommendation
