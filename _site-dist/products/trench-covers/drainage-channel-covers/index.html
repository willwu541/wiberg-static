Drainage channel covers (industrial)

Drainage channel covers with high open area for water flow. Designed for stormwater, surface drainage, and wastewater channels.

Request a Quote Downloads Product Spacing Guide

Product Description

Drainage channel covers are designed to maximize open area for water flow while providing a safe walking or driving surface over stormwater, surface drainage, and wastewater channels. They use grating with spacing optimized for hydraulic performance and debris passage. Suitable for industrial yards, municipal drainage systems, parking lots, and facility perimeter drainage. Available in welded construction with frame options and hot-dip galvanized or stainless finishes for corrosive environments.

Specification Details
Open area High open area for water flow; spacing per hydraulic requirements
Construction Welded steel grating; frame optional per channel design
Typical applications Stormwater, surface drainage, wastewater, industrial runoff
Materials Carbon steel, stainless steel (304, 316) for corrosive environments
Finishes Hot-dip galvanized, painted, stainless
Load Pedestrian to light vehicle; specify for correct selection

Frequently asked questions

Slotted vs mesh top?

Depends on debris tolerance and heel rules.

Locking?

Specify tamper resistance for public edges.

Material?

Galvanized steel typical; stainless near chemicals.

Maintenance?

Document lift weight for O&M manuals.

Combined hydraulic and traffic load?

If trucks can straddle the channel, state simultaneous hydraulic and structural checks.

Stainless frames?

Specify for aggressive chemical dikes or coastal yards.

Need a quotation?
