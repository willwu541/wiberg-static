Projects

Project proof and reference view. Evidence of delivery across energy, petrochemical, coal chemical, power generation, logistics, and infrastructure sectors.

220 kV Substation

Hot-dip galvanized bar grating platforms and cable trench covers for a 220 kV electrical substation. Products included G325/30/100 heavy-duty grating for transformer platforms, standard-duty walkways, and lockable trench covers for cable routing.

International Airport Terminal

Serrated anti-slip grating for landside drainage trenches and airside maintenance access platforms. All panels hot-dip galvanized to withstand de-icing chemicals and heavy ground support vehicle traffic.

Cathay Biotech Chemical Plant

Process platform flooring and reactor access walkways for a bio-based chemical manufacturing facility. Supplied close-mesh grating in corrosion-resistant finish for areas exposed to organic acid vapors.

Coal-to-Liquid Conversion Plant

Heavy-duty welded bar grating (G405/30/100) for elevated pipe rack platforms, maintenance walkways, and stair treads across a large-scale CTL facility. Panels designed for high point loads from maintenance equipment.

Coal Yard Pipeline Corridor

Standard-duty galvanized grating for pipeline corridor walkways and trench covers protecting underground utility lines. Panels fabricated with cutouts for pipe penetrations and banding at panel edges.

Polysilicon Production Facility

Platform flooring and chemical-area grating for a high-purity polysilicon manufacturing plant. Supplied press-locked grating for cleanroom-adjacent areas requiring smooth, flush surfaces.

Hyperscale Data Center

Ventilated raised floor panels and cable tray access grating for a hyperscale data center. Open-mesh design provides up to 70% airflow for underfloor cooling distribution while supporting server room rack loads.

Express Logistics Hub

Mezzanine floor grating and loading dock walkways for a high-throughput logistics sorting center. Serrated finish for forklift traffic areas, standard-duty for pedestrian zones.

Forestry Processing Facility

Galvanized steel grating platforms and stair systems for an outdoor forestry processing site. All panels specified for exposure to weathering, wood debris, and seasonal moisture.

Grain Storage & Logistics Park

Walkway grating and trench drainage covers for a grain storage and distribution facility. Anti-slip serrated grating specified for areas with grain dust and seasonal wet conditions.

Gulf Petrochemical Complex

Large-scale supply of hot-dip galvanized bar grating for a Middle East petrochemical complex. Products included platform flooring, pipe rack grating, stair treads, and heavy-duty trench covers across multiple process units.

100 kt/a Organic Amine Plant

Platform grating and reactor access structures for a 100,000-ton/year organic amine production facility. Close-mesh grating specified for areas with small-tool drop risk, standard welded grating for general walkways.

Nuclear Power Station

Heavy-duty galvanized bar grating for turbine hall platforms, cooling system access walkways, and cable trench covers. All materials supplied with full mill certificates and inspection documentation per nuclear quality requirements.

Propane-to-Propylene (PDH) Plant

Multi-level process platform grating for a propane dehydrogenation unit producing propylene. Supplied welded bar grating, stair treads, and toe plates for elevated reactor and column structures.

Xinjiang Polysilicon Complex

Large-area platform flooring for a polysilicon refining complex in Xinjiang. Standard and heavy-duty welded grating for outdoor process areas, with hot-dip galvanized finish for desert climate durability.

Polystyrene Production Plant

Process area grating and maintenance access platforms for a polystyrene resin manufacturing plant. Galvanized welded grating with serrated bearing bars for chemical-exposure walkways.

Thermal Power Station

Turbine hall platform grating, boiler house walkways, cable trench covers, and stair treads for a thermal power generation station. Heavy-duty G405/30/100 grating for equipment maintenance areas.
