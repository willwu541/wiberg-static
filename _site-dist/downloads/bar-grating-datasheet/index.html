Bar Grating Datasheet

Technical specification for welded, heavy-duty, press-locked, close-mesh, serrated, and ball-proof bar grating. Materials, spacing, finishes, and dimensional data for accurate specification.

Use this datasheet when specifying bar grating for walkways, platforms, mezzanines, or industrial flooring. Includes spacing formats (e.g. 19-W-4), material grades, and finish options.
