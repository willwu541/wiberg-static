Who We Are

Wiberg Metal focuses on practical, specification-driven manufacturing for project customers who require reliable product quality, stable fabrication capability, and responsive communication.

Our factory is equipped with automated welding lines, cutting equipment, and galvanizing capacity to deliver consistent quality at scale. We support custom fabrication to project drawings and specifications.

Production Process

From raw material to finished product. Our production flow ensures consistent quality and traceability.

1

Blanking

Raw steel cut to specification. Bearing and cross bars prepared per panel dimensions.

2

Shaping

Bars formed for assembly. Serrated bars receive surface treatment for anti-slip.

3

Welding

Automated pressure welders join bars at each intersection for load-bearing strength.

4

Cutting

Panels cut to final dimensions. Edge trimming to match project drawings.

5

Galvanizing

Hot-dip galvanizing for corrosion resistance. Zinc coating meets standards.

6

Quality Inspection

Dimensional checks, surface and weld verification. Certificates on request.

7

Packing

Export packing with protective wrapping. Bundles labeled for identification.

8

Warehouse & Shipping

Finished products stored and prepared for shipment. Documentation coordinated.
