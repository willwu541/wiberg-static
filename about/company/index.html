Company Profile

Wiberg Metal is a factory-direct manufacturer of steel grating, stair treads, trench covers, accessories, and FRP grating. We focus on specification-driven manufacturing for project customers who need reliable quality and responsive support.

Who We Are

With 18 years of manufacturing experience, we serve industrial and infrastructure projects worldwide. Our 20,000 m?production facility includes four production lines with robotic welding, delivering a processing capacity of 300,000 m?per year.

We work with engineers, contractors, and distributors who require clear specifications, consistent quality, and timely delivery. Our approach is practical: we respond to drawings and requirements, provide accurate quotations, and support projects through to delivery.

Key Facts

  • 18 years manufacturing experience
  • 20,000 m?production facility
  • 4 production lines including robotic welding
  • 300,000 m?/ year processing capacity
  • Export-oriented project support
