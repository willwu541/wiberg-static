INSTALLATION GUIDELINES (1/2)
  1. Check panel marks against layout — span direction must match shop drawings.
  2. Minimum bearing length typically 25 mm on steel; confirm on structural drawings.
  3. Install clips at manufacturer-recommended spacing; torque bolts evenly.
  4. Do not over-deflect panels during lift — use spreader bars on wide pieces.

INSTALLATION GUIDELINES (2/2)
  1. Banding bars and kick plates: weld sequence to control distortion — follow Wiberg WPS where supplied.
  2. After site cutting of galvanized work, repair zinc per ASTM A780 or project spec.
  3. Stainless: use isolated cutters/brushes; avoid carbon contamination.
  4. FRP: use stainless or GRP clips; follow resin supplier max torque.

GLOSSARY
Bearing barPrimary load-carrying bar, usually spanning between supports.
Cross barSecondary bar that holds bearing bars in position.
s₁ / s₂Centre-to-centre pitches of bearing bars and cross bars (mm).
HDGHot-dip galvanizing.
Press-weldedElectro-forged weld at each crossing.
Press-lockedMechanical lock of cross bar into bearing bar.
