Stair Tread Photo
STAIR TREADS
Safe access between levels.
• 6 types: welded/bolted, with/without nosing
• Checkered plate, abrasive, safety nosing
• Widths 125–325 mm, custom available
• Pre-drilled end plates, fast installation
• All grating types and materials
Trench Cover Photo
TRENCH & DRAIN COVERS
From pedestrians to 25-tonne trucks.
• Load-rated T-2 to T-25
• Side drains, cross drains, manholes
• Hinged, removable, or bolted
• Anti-theft hinge for manholes
• Support frames included
FRP Grating Photo
FRP GRATING
When steel can't survive the environment.
• Corrosion-proof: acids, alkalis, saltwater
• Lightweight: 1/3 weight of steel
• Electrically non-conductive
• Fire-retardant (ASTM E-84 Class 1)
• Molded and pultruded options
