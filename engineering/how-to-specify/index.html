How to Specify

What information to provide for RFQ and drawings to ensure accurate quotation and production.

Information to Provide

  • Product type: Bar grating, stair treads, trench covers, FRP, accessories
  • Spacing: e.g., 19-W-4, 15-W-2 (see Product Spacing Guide)
  • Material and finish: Galvanized, stainless, painted, FRP (see Materials and Finishes)
  • Panel size: Length –– width (mm or inches), bearing bar direction
  • Quantity: Number of panels or total area
  • Load/span: Required load capacity and span for verification
  • Drawings: Layout, dimensions, support details
  • Standards: Target standard if applicable (GB, ANSI/NAAMM, ASTM, EN/BS)
  • Delivery: Destination, port, incoterm

Drawing Requirements

Include dimensioned drawings where possible. Indicate bearing bar direction, span, support locations, and any special details (banding, nosing, toe plate, fixing details).
