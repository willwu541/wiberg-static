# Wiberg Industrial Catalog System

**Purpose:** Define a single, repeatable structure for Wiberg’s **printed and digital** technical sales material (master catalog, product datasheets, download pack).  
**Audience:** Specifiers, buyers, and fabricators in export markets.

**Reference use of third-party manuals (e.g. NAAMM, European producers):**  
Learn from **structure, terminology, and engineering logic** only. **Do not** copy wording, layouts, or table values. **Do not** imply products or capabilities Wiberg does not supply.

---

## 1. Product scope (hard boundary)

### 1.1 Wiberg supplies (only)

| Family | Includes |
|--------|----------|
| **Steel grating** | Forge-welded (pressure-welded) bar grating; press-locked steel bar grating |
| **Stair treads** | Fabricated treads based on the above (nosing, fixing, finishes as quoted) |
| **Trench covers** | Steel grating–based covers and frames as quoted |
| **Grating accessories** | Clips, banding, toe plates, joining / hold-down components aligned to above systems |
| **FRP grating** | Molded and/or pultruded FRP grating **only** where Wiberg actually lists and supplies |

### 1.2 Not supplied (must not appear as offered products)

- Aluminum bar grating  
- Expanded metal, perforated metal, wire mesh as **product lines**  
- Riveted bar grating, swage-locked systems, or any metal grating type **outside** welded / press-locked steel  
- Any category not **explicitly** confirmed in quotation

### 1.3 Industry context (allowed, controlled)

If mentioning other product types (e.g. “some projects specify aluminum grating”), use a **fixed disclaimer**, e.g.:

> *Wiberg does not supply aluminum bar grating, expanded metal, perforated metal, or wire mesh. We focus on welded and press-locked **steel** grating, associated treads and trench covers, accessories, and FRP grating where listed.*

Use **once** in an introductory or standards section—not repeated on every page.

## 2. Design principles (Western B2B catalog)

1. **Engineering-first:** Define terms, span direction, and support assumptions before load discussion.  
2. **One primary notation per section:** Present dimensions and spacing in **one clear system**; add a **short mapping** to other conventions only where it reduces RFQ errors (see §4).  
3. **No capability inflation:** State finishes, materials, and standards **as confirmed per project**; avoid blanket claims.  
4. **Traceable tables:** Any numeric load or span table must match **released Wiberg data** (tested, calculated, or third-party standard explicitly adopted for that document revision).  
5. **Actionable RFQ:** Every major section ends with **what to send** (dimensions, loads, standard, drawings).

| Document | Role |
|----------|------|
| **A. Master product catalog** | Overview, scope, terminology, families, typical configurations, RFQ guide, revision date |
| **B. Family datasheets** (PDF or web mirrors) | Steel grating, stair treads, trench covers, accessories, FRP—one logical family per file or section |

| **D. Company / quality one-pager** | Factory, QC, export support—**factual** only |

Avoid duplicating the same tables in five places; **cross-reference** with one authoritative location.

## 4. Standard and notation policy (avoid random mixing)

**Problem:** Mixing GB, NAAMM-style, and EN references in one paragraph confuses buyers.

**Policy:**

- **Body copy:** Use **SI units** (mm, kN/m², kPa) for international readability unless the document is **explicitly** labeled “US market edition.”  
- **Designation:** Prefer **Wiberg’s own product codes** or neutral descriptions (bearing bar size + bearing pitch + cross bar pitch + construction type).  
- **Mapping box (optional, one place per family):** e.g. “Common request equivalents” with **two columns max**—e.g. metric marking ↔ inch-based **naming** buyers use—**without** claiming certification to a standard unless true.  
- **Legal/compliance:** Name standards **only** when Wiberg **routinely** works to them **and** legal/commercial has approved the wording.

Do **not** paste unrelated standard clauses or load numbers from reference manuals.

## 5. Master catalog section order

Use this order unless a local market edition requires a small reorder:

1. **Cover & revision** — Document ID, date, contact, language.  
2. **Who we are** — One page: focus, geography, how to engage (short).  
3. **Product scope** — §1.1–1.3 above.  
4. **How to read this catalog** — Span direction, panel dimensions, what “quoted” means.  
5. **Terminology** — §6.  
6. **Steel grating** — Welded vs press-locked; plain/serrated; typical meshes; finishes; **RFQ checklist**.  
7. **Heavy-duty / close mesh / ball-proof** — As **variants** of steel grating, not separate unrelated lines.  
8. **Stair treads** — Types, nosing, fixing concepts, **assumptions** (support, load reference).  
9. **Trench covers** — Panel/frame concepts, duty classes **only if defined by Wiberg**.  
10. **Accessories** — Clip families, purposes, compatibility table (steel systems + FRP if applicable).  
11. **FRP grating** — Resin/types, applications, **separate** load logic from steel.  
12. **Installation & fixings overview** — Principles; detail drawings **as approved**.  
13. **Ordering & RFQ** — §9.  
14. **Appendices** — Load tables, approvals, packing—**only validated content**.

## 6. Global terminology (use consistently)

| Term | Definition (catalog usage) |
|------|----------------------------|
| **Bearing bar (load bar)** | Primary load-carrying flat bar; **span runs parallel to bearing bars** unless a drawing states otherwise. |
| **Cross bar** | Transverse bar that ties bearing bars; spacing stated **centre-to-centre** unless defined otherwise. |
| **Mesh / spacing** | Always give **bearing bar pitch** and **cross bar pitch** (c/c). |
| **Panel length × width** | State which dimension is **along bearing bars** (span direction). |
| **Banding** | Edge flat bar or trim; distinguish **trim** vs **structural** edge if relevant to your shop practice. |
| **Clear opening** | Opening size relevant to ball-proof or debris—define if you use it. |
| **Finish** | e.g. hot-dip galvanizing (state standard if claimed), paint system, stainless grade. |

Avoid ambiguous words like “mesh” without numbers.

## 7. Per-family content checklist

### 7.1 Steel grating (welded / press-locked)

- Construction type and **visual/process difference** (one short paragraph each).  
- Bearing bar: depth × thickness range **as offered**.  
- Spacing combinations **as offered**.  
- Surface: plain / serrated.  
- Materials: carbon steel grades **as offered**; stainless **as offered**.  
- Finishes: **as offered** with standard references only if verified.  
- Typical applications (bullet list)—**in scope only**.  
- **RFQ:** span, load or standard to meet, environment, drawing, quantity, destination.

### 7.2 Heavy-duty / close mesh / ball-proof

- Frame each as a **subset** of steel grating with **selection drivers** (load, opening limits, project rules).  
- Do **not** imply compliance with a regional rule unless **documented**.

### 7.3 Stair treads

- Tread types you sell; nosing options; fixing philosophy (welded vs bolted vs clip).  
- **Always** state: loads and spans are **project-specific** unless publishing **approved** tables.  
- Link to accessories.

### 7.4 Trench covers

- Frame types, cover types, **load class** only if Wiberg defines or maps them explicitly.  
- Clear statement: vehicular vs pedestrian projects need **confirmed** design input.

### 7.5 Accessories

- One table: **item** | **function** | **typical use** | **compatible grating types**.  
- No orphan clips without naming what they attach to.

### 7.6 FRP grating

- Separate from steel: resin, type (molded/pultruded), surface, chemical/creep caveats.  
- **Do not** reuse steel load tables for FRP.

---

## 8. Load tables and engineering claims

- **Rule:** If the number is not **released and maintained** by Wiberg, do not print it.  
- **Rule:** State **assumptions** next to every table: material, support, UDL vs point load, deflection limit, temperature, inclusion of self-weight.  
- **Rule:** Prefer “**request project verification**” over overstated generic spans.

---

## 9. Ordering & RFQ (catalog closing section)

Minimum request list (mirror on website):

- Product family and construction type  
- Bearing bar size and spacing; cross bar spacing  
- Panel sizes and **span direction**  
- Material and finish  
- Design loads / standard to meet (if any)  
- Quantity, delivery terms, drawings (PDF/DWG), required documents  

Optional: standard commercial fields (Incoterm, port) **if** sales approves.

---

## 10. Naming and visual consistency

- **Product names:** Title Case, no internal slug strings (e.g. avoid `composite-steel-grating` in customer-facing titles).  
- **Tables:** Same column order across families where possible: *Parameter | Value | Notes*.  
- **Icons/photos:** Real Wiberg or licensed assets; label **“Typical”** if not exact for every SKU.
