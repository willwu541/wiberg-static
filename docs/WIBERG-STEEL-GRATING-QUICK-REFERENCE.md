# Steel Grating Catalog — Quick reference (1 page)

*Detail, load assumptions, and exclusions: [`WIBERG-STEEL-GRATING-CATALOG-TEXT.md`](./WIBERG-STEEL-GRATING-CATALOG-TEXT.md). **Quotation and approved drawings govern.***

---

## Product types

- **Welded** — bearing and cross bars forge-welded at crossings; rigid grid; visible welds at crossings.  
- **Press-locked** — cross bars mechanically locked into bearing bars; no fusion weld at each crossing; smoother at crossings.  

**Steel only** in this program (welded + press-locked). **FRP** only if listed on the quotation.

---

## Key options

**Grid / panel**

- Bearing-bar size: depth × thickness (mm), as offered.  
- **s₁** — bearing-bar pitch (c/c, mm).  
- **s₂** — cross-bar pitch (c/c, mm); specify **with s₁**.  
- **Plain** or **serrated** top.  
- Panel length × width (mm); state **which dimension is parallel to bearing bars** (span direction).  
- Variants (if required): heavy-duty, close mesh, ball-proof (per contract criterion), custom cut/banding/cut-outs — **subject to quotation**.

**Materials**

- **Carbon steel** — default where corrosion is moderate or managed by coating/galvanizing; grades on quotation / mill docs.  
- **Stainless** — as quoted (e.g. 304/316 types); grade and environment per project.

**Finishes (carbon)**

- Hot-dip galvanizing; paint / coating to spec; duplex only with written system spec; **untreated** only for short exposure or immediate customer protection.  
- **Stainless:** normally uncoated.

## Typical applications

- Working platforms, maintenance walkways, open-grid flooring in process plants.  
- Trench and channel covers (industrial / municipal drainage).  
- Welded: industrial duty, higher traffic, stiffness-sensitive layouts.  
- Press-locked: cleaner grid at crossings; still verify capacity, deflection, supports.

**Sector overview (buyer orientation):** oil & gas, power plants, data centers, infrastructure, water treatment — **Section X — Applications** in [`WIBERG-STEEL-GRATING-CATALOG-TEXT.md`](./WIBERG-STEEL-GRATING-CATALOG-TEXT.md) / [HTML catalog](./WIBERG-STEEL-GRATING-CATALOG.html); does not replace quotation scope.

## Basic selection guidance

- Fix **loads, span in bearing-bar direction, supports, and deflection limits** before relying on type choice.  
- Use **released** Wiberg load/span data only under its stated assumptions; otherwise RFQ with drawings and load notes.  
- **Welded** — common default for conventional industrial flooring and straightforward support detailing.  
- **Press-locked** — consider when crossing appearance or no weld at each crossing is specified; **project checks** still required.  
- Finish: outdoor / general industrial on carbon → often HDG; aggressive or coastal → stainless or heavy coating **per spec / case review**.

## RFQ checklist

Send as a minimum:

- [ ] Construction: **welded** or **press-locked** (and variant if applicable).  
- [ ] Bearing bar **h × t** (mm); **s₁** and **s₂** (mm c/c).  
- [ ] **Plain** or **serrated**.  
- [ ] **Finish** (e.g. HDG, paint system, untreated, stainless grade).  
- [ ] Panel **L × W** (mm) + **span ∥ which dimension**.  
- [ ] Quantities; **banding**, **cut-outs**, accessories as relevant.  
- [ ] If structural check requested: **loads** (e.g. kN/m²), **deflection limit**, support/bearing notes.  
- [ ] Drawings: panel marks, grid orientation, cut-outs, support lines; cite material / welding / galvanizing / inspection specs; name required certificates up front.

**Also:** stair treads, trench covers, and FRP — separate datasheets / RFQ lists in addition to grating lines.

## Not in current steel grating scope

Aluminum bar grating; expanded / perforated / wire mesh as product families; riveted; swage-locked.
