How to Choose the Right Steel Grating for Your Project

Factors to consider when selecting welded bar grating, serrated grating, or press-locked grating for industrial platforms and walkways.

Selecting the right steel grating depends on load requirements, environment, drainage needs, and aesthetics. This guide covers the main types and when to use each.

Welded Bar Grating

Welded bar grating is the most common choice for general industrial platforms. It offers good load capacity, open drainage, and cost-effectiveness. Use it for equipment access, walkways, and elevated work areas where slip resistance is moderate.

Serrated Bar Grating

Serrated bar grating provides enhanced anti-slip performance. Choose it for wet, oily, or outdoor applications such as oil & gas platforms, marine facilities, and chemical plants.

Press-Locked Grating

Press-locked grating has a cleaner appearance with no weld marks. It suits architectural and aesthetic applications where appearance matters, such as façades and public areas.

Key Selection Factors

  • Load: Check span and uniform load—refer to load tables.
  • Environment: Galvanized for general use; stainless for corrosive environments.
  • Drainage: Open mesh allows drainage; specify spacing (e.g., 19-W-4, 15-W-2) per application.
  • Standards: Specify GB, ANSI/NAAMM, ASTM, or EN/BS as required.
